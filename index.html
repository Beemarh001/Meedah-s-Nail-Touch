Keep your set lovely

Simple aftercare.

A few small habits can help your manicure stay comfortable and looking fresh.

Use cuticle oil

Apply regularly to keep the skin around your nails feeling soft and hydrated.

Be gentle with your nails

Use your fingertips, not your nails, to open cans or scrape hard surfaces.

Wear gloves for cleaning

Household cleaners and long periods in water can be tough on polish and enhancements.

Book maintenance on time

For extensions, ask Meedah when to return for a refill based on your set and nail growth.
